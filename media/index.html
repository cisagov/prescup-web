PC7

The seventh annual President's Cup ran from January to May 2026. PC7 featured a bold spy theme that fused high-contrast minimalism with slick, shape-driven design, evoking the intrigue of espionage through modern execution. Inspired by classic spy tools and retro-futurist aesthetics, it included sleek, coded visuals that commanded attention and concealed layers beneath the surface.

PC6

The sixth annual President's Cup ran from January to April 2025 and featured an edgy cyberpunk theme that took competitors on a journey through a neon-drenched, high-tech metropolis. The Finals were held in person in Tysons Corner, Virginia and the Teams Finals included an Industrial Control Systems ICScape Room for the second year in a row that had the competitors test their luck to escape from a locked-down island compound.

PC5

The fifth annual President's Cup ran from January to April 2024 and featured an exciting theme centered around several beloved video games, with competitors showing off their best cyber moves and stretching their skills. The Finals were held in person at CISA facilities, and the Teams Finals included a new Industrial Control Systems ICScape Room. This interactive escape room experience immersed participants in a fictional storyline in which teams navigated through an intense scenario, completing various hands-on tasks such as finding hidden PLC equipment and installing a Ladder Logic and HMI display, to secure the scene and save the day.

PC4

The fourth annual President's Cup ran from August to December 2022 and had competitors blasting into orbit with a new space exploration theme. PC4 was packed with competitors from across the entire federal government, and the Finals were held in person for the first time in two years at CISA's Arlington facilities. The Teams Finals included a galaxy-traversing experience featuring sci-fi technology, aliens, and a race to restore balance to the universe.

PC3

The third annual President's Cup ran from August to December 2021 and featured a retro 1980's-inspired theme and all-new challenges based on in-demand NICE Framework Work Roles. PC3 saw a significant increase in participation from the first two competitions. The Finals were held virtually and livestreamed with live commentary from CISA's cybersecurity experts.

PC2

The second annual President's Cup Cybersecurity Competition ran from August 2020 to February 2021 and provided a new and improved experience for competitors. For the first time, Individuals were split into two tracks: Track A, focusing on defensive NICE Work Roles, and Track B, focusing on Offensive NICE Work Roles. The Teams Finals also included a new iteration of the escape room concept featuring a “Save the World” scenario in which teams responded to a weather-based crisis at a local municipality as part of a Disaster Relief Task Force (DRTF).

PC1

The inaugural President's Cup Cybersecurity Competition ran from September to December 2019, and drew more than 1,000 individuals and 200 teams. After two Qualifying Rounds, 10 Individuals finalists and five Teams finalists came to the Washington, D.C., area for the Finals at the CISA Cybersecurity Lab. The five teams were given a series of challenges to solve on the first day, followed by a virtual escape room on the second day. During the escape scenario, teams were given 8 hours to solve a series of challenges from forensics to exploitation, culminating in gaining a password to an elevator to "escape."
